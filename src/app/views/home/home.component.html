Protein Ensemble Database

PED is a freely available, manually curated repository for depositing, sharing and analyzing structural ensembles of intrinsically disordered proteins and regions (IDPs/IDRs).

It integrates data from NMR SAXS smFRET MD to support ensemble validation and advance modeling methodologies.

Examples: P38634 (SIC1) PED00001 Q89933 (MeV)
